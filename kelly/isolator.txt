Kelly UART galvanic isolator — build plan
=========================================

Goal: an OBD-powered RejsaCAN reads the Kelly with the same ~99% frame
success as a floating USB-powered board.

Why
---
The Kelly KLS7218M is powered from the traction pack, so its V- is not at
chassis potential. The RejsaCAN, powered from the OBD port, is
chassis-referenced (and must stay that way for CAN). Wiring the two grounds
together injects the chassis-to-traction noise into the UART's signal
reference and corrupts frames whenever the board runs on tractor power
(see README.md "Grounding dominates reliability"). A galvanic isolator
removes the ground connection entirely: the Kelly side floats with the
traction pack, the board side stays on chassis, and signals cross an
isolation barrier instead of sharing a reference.

The isolator also replaces the two level-shift hacks: run its Kelly side at
5 V and it natively reads the Kelly's 5 V-TTL Tx and drives the Kelly's Rx
at a full 5 V, while the board side regenerates everything at 3.3 V. The
~1-2.2 kOhm series resistor on the RX line is no longer needed.

Isolator options
----------------
Any dual-channel digital isolator with one channel in each direction, in
SOIC-8, pins 1..8 = VDD1/VOA/VIB/GND1/GND2/VOB/VIA/VDD2. Take the
default-output-HIGH variant of whichever part — the key-off behavior note
at the bottom of this file depends on it. All of these are happy at 5 V on
the Kelly side and 3.3 V on the board side, and all are far faster than
the link's 19200 Bd needs. Prices Aug 2026, qty 1-5:

  - NOVOSENSE NSi8221N1-DSPR — $1.08 at DigiKey, US stock. 3.75 kV,
    150 Mbps, 200 kV/us CMTI, AEC-Q100. The pick if you want one this
    week without an international order.
  - 2Pai Semi pi122U31 — $0.26, LCSC/JLCPCB only, no US distribution.
    3.0 kV, 150 kbps (still 8x headroom at 19200 Bd), 250 kV/us. Cheapest
    by far, and effectively free riding along on a JLCPCB board order.
  - Chipanalog CA-IS3722HS — $0.22 at LCSC. 3.75 kV, 150 Mbps, Schmitt
    inputs, 5 ns min pulse. Best of the lot on paper, but was out of
    stock when checked.
  - ADI ADuM1201ARZ — $4.82, the original. 2.5 kV, 1 Mbps, 25 kV/us: the
    worst specs here at ~5x the price. Kept only because the board and
    these docs are written around its pinout.
  - NVE IL721-3E — $8.05 at DigiKey, fabbed in Minnesota. Only if US
    manufacture is the requirement: its datasheet defines NO default
    output state for an unpowered input side, so key-off would be
    undefined rather than idle-high.

Every vendor numbers the three channel arrangements differently, so the
part numbers rhyme and mean opposite things. The middle column below is
verified against each vendor's own datasheet pin table; check anything
not on this list the same way before ordering.

  vendor        2 fwd / 0 rev    1/1, ADuM1201        1/1, mirrored
                (cannot UART)    pin order (USE)      (not a drop-in)
  ADI           ADuM1200         ADuM1201             --
  2Pai Semi     pi120U31         pi122U31             pi121U31
  NOVOSENSE     NSi8220          NSi8221              NSi8222
  Chipanalog    CA-IS3720        CA-IS3722            CA-IS3721
  NVE           IL711            IL721                IL712

Isolated-side power: 12 V -> 5 V
--------------------------------
The isolated (Kelly) side needs a supply referenced to the Kelly's V-.
That is what SM-4P pin 1 (red, ~12 V out of the controller) is for — it is
how Kelly's own Bluetooth dongle powers itself. Never wire the 12 V to the
isolator directly; regulate it down:

  - 78L05 (TO-92) + 0.33 uF ceramic on IN and 0.1 uF on OUT, both right at
    the regulator. Load is a few mA, so dissipation is ~40 mW — no heat
    sink. A TO-220 LM7805 (or L7805CV) works with the same two caps if
    that is what is in the drawer.
  - Do not use a switching buck module: at 5 mA there is no efficiency
    argument, and it puts converter hash next to the UART lines.
  - Optional but useful: LED + 1 kOhm across the 5 V rail. It lights only
    when the controller is powering pin 1, so it doubles as a
    "Kelly is awake" indicator.

Wiring
------
isolator.svg draws all of this out, including the passives.

        KELLY SIDE (floating)      | barrier |      BOARD SIDE (chassis)
                                   |         |
 red ---> 78L05 ---> 5V ---> VDD1 -| ADuM    |- VDD2 <--- 3V3
 blue  (Kelly Rx) <--------- VOA  -|  1201   |- VIA  <--- GPIO48 (TX)
 green (Kelly Tx) ---------> VIB  -| breakout|- VOB  ---> GPIO47 (RX)
 black --------------------> GND1 -|         |- GND2 <--- GND

Rows above are in physical pin order: 1-2-3-4 down side 1, 8-7-6-5 down
side 2.

Pin map (ADuM1201 datasheet Rev. L Figure 5 — side 1 carries VOA/VIB, side 2
VOB/VIA; channel A runs board->Kelly, channel B Kelly->board):

  Kelly side (isolated, side 1)        Board side (chassis, side 2)
  VDD1 (1) <- 5 V from regulator       VDD2 (8) <- RejsaCAN 3V3
  VOA  (2) -> Kelly blue (Rx), output  VIA  (7) <- GPIO48 (TX), input
  VIB  (3) <- Kelly green (Tx), input  VOB  (6) -> GPIO47 (RX), output
  GND1 (4) <- Kelly black (V-), NOT 2  GND2 (5) <- board GND

**GND1 is pin 4, at the bottom of side 1.** Side 1 is NOT the mirror image
of side 2 -- assuming it was put the wrong pinout on the rev A PCB. Anything
deriving pin numbers from this file must use the table above, not the
row order of a schematic sketch.

There is no longer any wire between Kelly V- and board GND, and no series
resistor anywhere on the link.

Note the ADuM1201's input thresholds scale with each side's own VDD, so
the Kelly side must actually be powered at 5 V; at 3.3 V it would
under-drive the Kelly's Rx again.

Cabling
-------
Twist the pairs in the Kelly-side cable (green+black, blue+red), keep that
run short, and route it away from the pump phase leads. The board-side run
is noise-immune by construction. Label both ends (KELLY / REJSACAN) — the
dongle looks symmetric.

Bill of materials (~$10)
------------------------
  - the PCB from isolator-pcb/ (2-layer, 50 x 32 mm)
  - isolator chip: bare SOIC-8 soldered directly to the board
  - 78L05 (or LM7805) + 0.33 uF + 0.1 uF ceramics
  - LED + 1 kOhm resistor
  - twisted hookup wire for both runs; enclosure options are in
    isolator-pcb/README.md

Behavior notes
--------------
  - The ADuM1201 is fail-safe high: with the tractor off (Kelly side
    unpowered) GPIO47 sees a clean idle-high line, not garbage, so the
    firmware's existing stale-out handles key-off with no code changes.
  - Verification: flash the dashboard firmware (-DENABLE_KELLY
    -DKELLY_DEBUG), power the board from the OBD port, run the pump, and
    watch /json kelly_dbg — frames_ok should climb at ~99% of polls, as it
    does on floating USB power.
